Mower Math

The box mows an acre of brochure grass, the pack counts watt-hours it will never hold, and the charger counts its hours in weekends.

Open the app →

The battery truth

Sticker volts are the charger-off peak, not the working voltage - the honest watt-hours are volts times amp-hours times the 0.9 norm (labeled). Divided by the grass thirst norm, they name the square feet the pack really mows. The 40-volt, 4 amp-hour pack on ordinary grass is in reach on a short, dry day.

The pace truth

A deck and a walking speed make a theoretical ribbon of cut; the 0.85 overlap norm (labeled) trims it to the pace you actually walk, and the lawn's square feet name the minutes.

The charge truth

Watt-hours divided by charger watts, times the 1.15 loss norm (labeled), is the wait before the next mow - after lunch, tomorrow, or next weekend.

What it never does

It never counts the peak volts as working volts, never reads the brochure acre as your lawn, and never forgets the norms are labeled - the unmowed strip along the far fence keeps the honest ledger.

Mower Math is open source - the engine is small enough to read, and the math is plain enough to argue with.
